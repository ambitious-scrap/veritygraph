# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical researchers, analysts, and builders checking claims and AI-generated answers under time pressure. The primary job is to turn a query or pasted answer into a compact, auditable evidence record.

## Product Purpose

VerityGraph extracts atomic claims, retrieves supporting and challenging evidence, and compiles a pass / warning / fail verification result. Success means a researcher can see what is supported, what is uncertain, what would change a verdict, and where each source came from without leaving the workspace.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, direct. The product should feel trustworthy and precise without theatrics. Every important statement should point to evidence or clearly state uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI chat styling, noisy dashboard decoration, marketing-heavy claims, unexplained jargon, competing primary actions, and dense card nesting that hides the evidence trail.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the verification task, not product explanation.
- Make uncertainty and contradiction visible, not softened.
- Show evidence close to the claim it supports or challenges.
- Use progressive disclosure for source detail and confidence factors.
- Prefer plain language and one obvious next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the product workspace. Preserve keyboard-visible focus, semantic labels, readable contrast, touch-friendly controls, responsive layouts from 375px upward, and `prefers-reduced-motion` behavior. Never rely on verdict color alone; pair semantic colors with text and icons.
